About Nolan

Built for rooms that need more than inspiration.

Nolan speaks from the operating layer. His work is not about generic AI optimism. It is about what it takes to build the robots, edge systems, infrastructure, talent pipelines, and technical institutions that carry intelligence into the physical world.

Short Bio

Nolan S. Code is a physical AI speaker and the founder and executive director of the Atlanta AI & Robotics Initiative, a year-round nonprofit building robotics, edge AI, infrastructure, and workforce pathways for underserved students through Morehouse College and the Atlanta University Center. A former Microsoft AI and cloud specialist, U.S. Army veteran, and Georgia Tech MBA, Nolan has helped train more than 40 students, raised more than $100,000 for the mission, and built a model that helped its first graduate secure a $115,000 infrastructure role.

Longer Bio

Nolan’s work sits at the intersection of physical AI, robotics, edge systems, data centers, energy, and workforce development. Through AARI, he is building a practical talent pipeline that trains underserved students to become operators, not observers. His work includes two live data center training environments, edge AI demonstrations, robotics and autonomous-navigation education, technical certification pathways, and partnerships with major technology companies and academic institutions. He recently delivered the opening keynote and joined the fireside conversation at TAG //SHIFT: AI 2026, following appearances at RenderATL and the Georgia AI Data Center Leadership Summit. His core message is simple: the physical AI economy needs operators, not observers.

Advisory

Strategic Advisory

For institutions that need to stop reacting to AI and start operating it.

Most organizations do not have a physical AI vision problem. They have an infrastructure, workforce, and operating-model problem. Advisory engagements give leadership teams a clear read on where they are, what to build, and who to train, backed by named deliverables and a defined timeline.

Executive Briefing

A focused half-day or full-day session with the leadership team. Deliverable: a written readiness assessment specific to the organization's context, talent base, and stated goals. For boards, cabinets, and executive teams that need a shared operating picture before committing to a strategy.

Value floor: $30,000.

Readiness Sprint

A six to eight week engagement co-authored with an executive sponsor. Deliverable: a strategy document covering skills taxonomy, lab and infrastructure build-out, partner architecture, curriculum-to-jobs map, and an eighteen-month roadmap. Weekly working sessions. Named outputs.

Value floor: $100,000.

Annual Advisory

A standing advisory relationship for institutions making multi-year commitments to AI infrastructure and workforce development. Quarterly on-site, monthly strategic calls, board-level support, and named affiliation. Limited to four clients per year.

Value floor: $300,000.

Advisory engagements are value-based and scoped around executive priority, institutional complexity, deliverables, travel, preparation, and strategic impact. Booking details are in the contact section.

Before Booking

Scope the room before we scope the work.

To scope the engagement properly, please include the following:

  • Event date
  • Event location
  • Virtual, in-person, or hybrid
  • Expected audience size
  • Audience type
  • Desired format
  • Strategic outcome expected from the session
  • Whether the session will be recorded, streamed, distributed, or reused
  • Available budget range
  • Travel coverage
  • Point of contact for contract and payment
