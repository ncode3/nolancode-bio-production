About Nolan

Experience from the cloud to the classroom.

Nolan S. Code is a physical AI keynote speaker and founder of the Atlanta AI & Robotics Initiative. He connects robotics, edge systems, data centers, and workforce development through practical experience building hands-on training programs.

A former Microsoft AI and cloud specialist, U.S. Army veteran, Morehouse College alumnus, and Georgia Tech MBA, Nolan helps audiences understand the infrastructure and people behind AI deployment.
