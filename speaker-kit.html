Bio

Nolan S. Code is a physical AI speaker and the Founder and CEO of the Atlanta AI & Robotics Initiative, a year-round nonprofit building robotics, edge AI, infrastructure, and workforce pathways for underserved students through Morehouse College and the Atlanta University Center. AARI has trained more than 40 students, raised more than $100,000, established two live training environments, and helped its first graduate secure a $115,000 infrastructure role.
