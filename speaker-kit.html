Bio

Nolan S. Code is a physical AI keynote speaker and the Founder and CEO of the Atlanta AI & Robotics Initiative (AARI), a nonprofit training underserved students to build and operate the infrastructure behind AI, from robotics and edge systems to data centers. He champions local AI: running models on your own hardware, controlling your data, and moving beyond demos into production. A U.S. Army veteran, Morehouse College graduate, and Georgia Tech MBA, Nolan founded IntelHP and sold it to AWS, and at Microsoft closed a $100M Azure renewal at 174% of quota. AARI has trained 40+ students, raised $100K+, and placed its first graduate in a $115K infrastructure role. He delivered the opening keynote at TAG //SHIFT: AI 2026 and keynoted RenderATL 2026. His message is simple: own the stack.
